About

Mahendra Singh Dhoni (born 7 July 1981, Ranchi) is a former captain of the Indian national cricket team, widely regarded as one of the most successful captains and a great finisher in limited-overs cricket. He led India to victory in the inaugural ICC T20 World Cup (2007) and the ICC Cricket World Cup (2011).

Legacy

MS Dhoni's legacy extends beyond his records and achievements. He is celebrated for his calm demeanor under pressure, innovative captaincy, and ability to finish matches. Dhoni has inspired a generation of cricketers and remains a beloved figure in Indian cricket.
